_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use Bellows Cloud (the hosted service at `bellows.cloud` and `app.bellows.cloud`) and this documentation site. It is written for the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Who is responsible

Bellows Cloud is operated by **[OPERATOR LEGAL NAME]**, **[REGISTERED ADDRESS]** ("Bellows", "we"). Contact: [support@bellows.cloud](mailto:support@bellows.cloud).

We act in two roles:

- **Controller** for account data, usage and billing records, security logs and support messages.
- **Processor** for the content your organization puts into Bellows: task text, attachments, repository content, logs and transcripts. Your organization decides what is sent to Bellows and is the controller for it. Requests about that content should go to your organization's admin first.

### Self-hosted Bellows

Bellows is open-source software. If you use a self-hosted deployment, the person or company running it is responsible for your data, and this policy does not apply. A self-hosted deployment sends us no data.

## What we collect

| Data | Examples | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | GitHub user id, GitHub login, display name, avatar URL, first and last sign-in time | GitHub, when you sign in |
| Organization and membership | Organization name, GitHub App installation, members, invited GitHub logins, roles | You and your admins |
| Sessions and access tokens | A session cookie, hashes of access tokens you create, CLI and MCP authorizations | Created when you sign in or create a token |
| Task content | Task text, questions and answers, attached files, run logs, agent transcripts, checkpoints, gate results, pull request links, the runner's working copy of your repository | You, your repository and the agent's run |
| Settings | Variables, secrets, executor configurations, model provider keys, connections, notification webhooks, skills, workflows | You and your admins |
| Connected accounts | Access tokens for the services you connect (for example Jira or Linear) and the account identifier that service returns, which can be a name or email address | The connected service, when you authorize it |
| Usage | Run minutes and token counts per run, who started it and on which repository | Generated by the service |
| Audit log | Who did which administrative action and when | Generated by the service |
| Technical logs | IP address, user agent, request time and path, error details | Your browser, CLI or client |
| Support | The content of messages you send us and your contact address | You |

We do not ask for special categories of personal data. Do not put them into tasks, attachments or repositories that Bellows works on.

## Why we use it, and the legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6) |
| --- | --- |
| Provide the service: sign you in, run tasks, open pull requests, send the notifications you set up | Performance of a contract (1)(b) |
| Enforce plan limits, measure usage and bill | Performance of a contract (1)(b); legal obligation (1)(c) for accounting records |
| Keep the service secure, prevent abuse, find and fix faults | Legitimate interests (1)(f) |
| Answer support requests | Performance of a contract (1)(b); legitimate interests (1)(f) |
| Comply with the law and respond to lawful requests | Legal obligation (1)(c) |

We do not sell personal data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use your task content or repository content to train AI models. We make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## Cookies

The dashboard sets one session cookie, which is strictly necessary to keep you signed in. Bellows Cloud sets no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. This documentation site is served by a hosting provider that may set its own technical cookies.

## Who receives data

We share personal data only with the recipients below, and only as far as the purpose needs.

| Recipient | What and why |
| --- | --- |
| GitHub | Sign-in, reading and pushing to the repositories your organization installed the GitHub App on, opening pull requests |
| AI model providers (for example Anthropic or OpenAI, or the provider set in your executor) | Task text, repository content and tool results, sent by the agent during a run. With your own provider key, the provider handles that data under your agreement with it |
| Services you connect (for example Atlassian Jira, Linear, Discord) | What the integration needs: reading and updating issues, or posting task events to your channel |
| Cloud hosting (Google Cloud) | Runs the service and stores its database, files and backups |
| Operational monitoring (Grafana Cloud) | Service metrics, traces and server logs |
| Documentation hosting (Mintlify) | Serves this documentation site |
| Billing provider, where you buy a paid plan | Billing contact and payment details. We do not store card numbers |
| Authorities or advisers | Where the law requires it, or to establish or defend legal claims |

Providers that act on our behalf are bound by data processing agreements.

### International transfers

Some recipients process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on an adequacy decision of the European Commission or on its standard contractual clauses. Ask us for a copy at [support@bellows.cloud](mailto:support@bellows.cloud).

## How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Task content, logs, transcripts, attachments, usage telemetry | Your plan's window, then deleted. See [Data retention](/guides/retention) |
| Settings, secrets, connections, members | Until you remove them or the organization is deleted |
| Audit log | As long as the organization exists |
| Usage records used for billing | As long as accounting and tax law require. They hold no prompt, log or code |
| Account | Until you ask us to delete it |
| Technical logs and backups | Until they expire on their own schedule. Deleted data can remain there until then |

When an organization is deleted, its tasks, logs, transcripts, settings, stored files and secrets are purged and cannot be restored. See [Organization and plan](/guides/organization).

## Security

Secrets, provider keys, connection tokens and webhook URLs are stored encrypted and are not shown again after saving. Access tokens are stored as hashes. Each run executes in an isolated runner, and each organization's data is separated from every other organization's. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once at [support@bellows.cloud](mailto:support@bellows.cloud) if you suspect a problem.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

- give you access to your personal data and a copy of it;
- correct data that is wrong;
- delete your data;
- restrict how we use it, or object to use based on our legitimate interests;
- give you your data in a portable format.

Write to [support@bellows.cloud](mailto:support@bellows.cloud). We answer within one month. An organization admin can download the organization's data at any time with the [organization export](/guides/export). Where we act as a processor, we pass your request to the organization that controls the data.

You can also complain to the data protection authority of the EU country where you live or work, or to **[LEAD SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY]**.

## Children

Bellows is a tool for professional software teams. It is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes to this policy

We update the date at the top when this policy changes. If a change is significant, we tell organization admins in the dashboard or by another direct channel before it takes effect.
